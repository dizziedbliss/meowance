meowance is a free student project by Max for students of Malnad College of Engineering (MCE). it is not run by, or affiliated with, MCE or the Contineo portal. by using it you agree to the below.

use your own login

log in only with your own USN and birthday. don't use other people's, and don't try to guess them.

the numbers can be wrong

everything comes from the college portal, which is often out of date, and meowance does math on top of it. bunk counts are predictions, not promises: teachers may not take or enter every class. the official record is the college's, not ours. use your own judgment, and don't blame the cat if you land in the fine zone.

notes you share

  • only upload notes you made yourself or have the right to share. no textbooks or paid material you don't have the right to share, and nothing harmful, illegal or someone else's private information.
  • you're responsible for what you upload. anyone with a note's link can open it.
  • Max can remove any note, and block anyone who abuses the site, without notice.
  • if you think a note breaks your rights, report it with the report button or the "tell max" box and it will be looked at.

don't break it

no automated scraping, no hammering the server, no trying to get around the limits. the portal and the hosting are shared with other students.

no guarantees

meowance is provided as is, with no warranty. it may change, break, or shut down at any time, and the portal may block it. to the extent the law allows, Max isn't liable for anything that happens from using it.
